# Flagstone website

Public site for Flagstone by Monaidea, served by GitHub Pages from `main` at https://flagstone.monaidea.com (custom domain in `CNAME`; the owner creates the DNS record). Same look as https://ledgerstone.monaidea.com (repo VestaNabu-LLC/ledgerstone-site): `style.css` and `site.js` are copies of that site's files, with a few Flagstone additions at the end of `style.css`.

Flagstone is in development (repo VestaNabu-LLC/flagstone, its `CLAUDE.md` holds the rules). Until the owner decides otherwise, the site says "in development", shows no price and no store link, and has no privacy policy page. Every feature on the page is built (v2 checked by the owner on 2026-10-25); keep the page true to the app's `SPEC.md` and the Done items in its `owner/requests.md`, and mark anything not built yet as coming. Never claim something the app does not do yet. VestaNabu LLC appears only in the copyright line.

Contact form (owner request, 2026-11-02): `/support/` has the same form as https://www.monaidea.com, and the site's other calls to action link to it (`/support/#contact`, with `?topic=early-access` and similar to preselect a topic). `contact.js` is a copy of the file in VestaNabu-LLC/monaidea; keep the three copies (monaidea, ledgerstone-site, flagstone-site) identical and bump `?v=` on its script tag when it changes. The form posts to Monaidea's contact Worker at https://forms.monaidea.com (source and tests in the monaidea repo's `worker/`), which checks Cloudflare Turnstile and emails support@monaidea.com through Postmark; `data-product` on the form puts the app's name in the email's subject.
