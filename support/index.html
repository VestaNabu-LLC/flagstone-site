Flagstone support

Send us a message and include your Confluence site address (for example, yourcompany.atlassian.net).

We use your name, email and message only to answer you. The form goes through Cloudflare, which checks that you are not a bot, and Postmark, which delivers it to us. Privacy Policy

Prefer email, or want to send a screenshot? Write to support@monaidea.com.

Before you write

  • Availability: install Flagstone from the Atlassian Marketplace: free for up to 10 users, with a 30-day free trial.
  • What it changes: nothing. Flagstone only reads Confluence; it never ticks tasks, resolves comments or edits pages.
  • What you see: only what Confluence already lets you see. Tasks and comments on pages you cannot open never appear.

Please never send

Passwords, API tokens or exports of your pages or user list. We will never ask for them.
